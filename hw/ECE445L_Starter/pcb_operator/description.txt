This pcb board is responsible for hosting the pedals, the steering wheel, and the speaker to our driving simulator. We intend the LCD to display the gear shift and the current speed of the RC car. The rotary potentiometers on the top of the PC board (VR2, VR3)will be used for the pedals, the rotary potentiometer (VR1) will be used for the steering wheel. The ESP32 will send information to the RC car and update the speed/direction of the car. The buttons on the pcb will shift the gear of the car and will likely be hooked up to some paddles behind the steering wheel. We also added both the SMD TM4C and the launchpad just in case we can't get the SMD working properly. Some of the components we plan to hang off of the pcb board to save pcb area (we were wondering if this was ok or if we should change this).
